Reading the five together: Figure 1 shows the site's mass concentrated in its interface pages; Figure 2 shows what the sitemap alone would have missed (crawl-only pages, notably deep-linked blog posts and navigation pages); Figure 3 shows the corpus of skills to be broad rather than spiky — most pages yield a substantial body; Figure 4 shows that the site is a densely cross-linked single work rather than isolated silos, which is why section-organized skills quote links outward rather than duplicating content; and Figure 5 quantifies exactly how much honest content the augmentation layer recovered from what static scraping alone would have rendered as shells.
